    What leaves this page: your words go to Claude only when you choose "Read with Claude", and to another model only if you paste them there yourself. Everything else (the checks, the drawing and the package) is worked out here, in your browser. Your work is saved to your own private space, which only you can read, or to this browser when that is not available. "Start a new intent" clears it.
