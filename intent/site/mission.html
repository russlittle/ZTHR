Mission control

The front office: it runs the plant. It sets priorities, gives approvals, stops the line when it must and holds the spend, and it sees every product line and product, where each stands in each area, and what waits on a person. The measure that counts is journeys walked as a person would, and passing.

What mission control may do

  • Set priorities for every area: which product line and which product the plant works on next.
  • Give approvals: the acts only a person may do, such as signing an intent and approving production, wait here.
  • Stop the line: a red required check stops new work on its branch until the fix names it. See tools/stop-line.ts and src/governance/stopline.ts.
  • Hold the spend: each crew works within a budget, shown on Maintenance; a budget per product is not recorded yet.

Its lamps are readings, never inferred: an area with nothing measured says so.
