A team that works for every student, every day.

Student Advocate runs a student's day, finds the money and help they qualify for, and does the legwork to get it. It belongs to the student, it speaks plainly, and it never shares anything unless they say so.

What it does

Eight things a good advisor, a benefits navigator and a well-organized friend would do, all at once, by app or by text.

Runs your day

Classes, shifts, child care and due dates on one line. It holds time for the work that matters and tells you the one thing to do today.

Finds money you are missing

Checks SNAP, phone and heating help, and campus emergency grants against what you told it, using the agencies' own published limits.

Finds help and does the legwork

Food, rent, power, child care, legal help: verified, current programs near you, what to bring, and a drafted message you can send.

Handles campus life

What is open to eat right now, free food today, a quiet room booked and confirmed, and when to leave for the bus with live delays.

Keeps up with your classes

Reads your syllabus and your class site, moves a date when your instructor moves it, and plans the work in the time you have.

Opens doors to work

Jobs that fit around your classes and shifts, a fair-chance path when you ask for it, and an introduction to a mentor in your field.

Keeps you enrolled

Your grade so far and what the final needs, whether you keep your financial aid, and every choice about dropping a class side by side, with the aid that would go back.

Brings in your advisor, your way

A read-only link you control: only what you choose to show, for as long as you choose, and you see each time it is opened.

How it works

A small team of specialist agents behind one friendly voice, with every step a student can see.

  1. Tell it onceBy text or in the app: your classes, your shifts, what is hard right now. Or forward the email and it reads it.
  2. It plans and checksThe planner, money coach and help finder work from rules and verified sources, and a tone check makes sure every message is kind and has a next step.
  3. You decideAnything that would leave on your behalf waits for your yes on the exact words. Bookings are confirmed with the room system before it says they are done.

Why it works

Students stop out over a few hundred dollars, a missed form or a bus that never came. Help exists for most of it; finding it, qualifying and doing the paperwork is the barrier. The Advocate removes the barrier, and earns trust by how it behaves.

Nothing leaves without you

Every message it writes for you waits for your approval of the exact words. A tap on Send is the only way anything goes out.

Rules before guesses

Eligibility, open hours and bus times are computed from published sources, not guessed by a model. When it does not know, it says so.

Your facts stay yours

Sensitive facts are sealed with your own key. You can see, export or delete everything, any time, in one step.

Kind, plain and short

It never scolds. Every message offers a next step, at the reading level and length you chose.

Crisis comes first

Words that mean someone is in danger go straight to 988 or 911 information, before anything else happens.

Checked every build

More than a thousand tests, every button walked for every student, attacks people really send, and a trace of where your data can go.

What it is worth

For students

  • Money they qualify for and were not getting, found in minutes
  • Time held for the work that matters, around shifts and child care
  • Fewer missed deadlines and fewer surprise bills
  • Help that comes to them, in their words, with no judgment

For colleges

  • A support layer that reaches every student, at night and on weekends
  • Outcome measures that never show a group smaller than five
  • Any college added with one file; local help kept fresh automatically
  • No model spend in the product; costs measured in cents

For the field

  • A complete, open reference built by AI under fixtures
  • Every block reusable, every test public, every decision recorded
  • Proof that zero-trust AI development produces software people can trust
